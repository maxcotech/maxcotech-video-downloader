MAXCOTECH VIDEO DOWNLOADER - HOW TO USE
=======================================

Open it from the Start menu (or the desktop icon): "Maxcotech Video Downloader".

FIRST TIME
  The app asks where to save your videos. Pick any folder you like.
  You can change it any time: Settings > Save to > Browse...

ZOOM RECORDINGS
  1. Paste your class messages into the top box - as many as you like at once:

        Recording for Aug 8
        https://us06web.zoom.us/rec/share/....
        Passcode: Hx4=+tR9

     One-per-line also works:   <link> <passcode>   or   <link>,<passcode>
  2. Click "Add to queue and download" (or press Ctrl+Enter).
  3. Each recording is named after the title line above its link.

YOUTUBE
  1. Pick a quality under Settings > YouTube quality:
        Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p, or Audio only (MP3).
     The quality is remembered for each link when you add it.
  2. Paste video links (youtube.com/watch..., youtu.be/..., Shorts) or a
     playlist / channel link. A playlist becomes one row per video.
  3. Files are named after the video's title.
  Only download videos you have the right to save (your own uploads, Creative
  Commons videos, or material the owner allows you to keep). Private,
  members-only and age-restricted videos aren't supported.

SPEED SETTINGS
  Connections per video   - default 16. Each video is split into pieces that
                            download in parallel. Raise it on fast internet.
                            (YouTube links use at most 8, as YouTube limits more.)
  Videos at the same time - default 3.

GOOD TO KNOW
  * Pause/resume any time. Closing the app is safe: unfinished downloads continue
    from where they stopped next time you open it.
  * Right-click a row: open file, show in folder, pause, retry, copy link,
    show error details, remove.
  * Double-click a finished row to play it; double-click a failed row to see why.
  * "Wrong or missing passcode"  -> check the passcode, remove the row, paste again.
  * "Access denied / not found"  -> the link has expired or the host blocked it.
  * "YouTube is asking to confirm you're not a bot" -> wait a while, then Retry,
    and download fewer videos at the same time.
  * The app keeps its download engine up to date by itself (checked once a day).
  * Please respect your school's / host's rules about class recordings.

WINDOWS SAID "Windows protected your PC" WHEN INSTALLING?
  The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows doesn't
  recognise it. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".
